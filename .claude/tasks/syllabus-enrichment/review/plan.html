The syllabus says more than the game teaches

I downloaded the four official NIE ICT syllabi and mapped every competency level against all 86 activities in the game. Everything Ishini and Dilini built is on-syllabus and correct. But about a quarter of the prescribed content has no activity at all, and one grade 8 lesson teaches an O/L topic in place of its own. Five decisions below, then I build.

First, the good news

Every one of the 25 lessons maps onto a real NIE competency. There is no invented content, no padding, and no topic that belongs in another grade, apart from the single grade 8 case below. The lesson order follows the syllabus order. This is a gap-filling job, not a correction job.

And the 2018 syllabus is still the live one

The junior secondary reform moved from 2026 to January 2027 (Cabinet, 13 January 2026). The Ministry notice told schools that grade 6 students "should be taught the previous syllabus" and that last year's textbooks are reused through 2026. A parliamentary subcommittee confirmed the 2027 date on 14 September 2026, six days ago. Grades 7 to 9 were never in the 2026 phase at all. So these four PDFs are what is actually being taught in class right now, and building against them is safe for at least this academic year.

2

The grade 8 finding, and what it settles

This is the one place where the game teaches something the syllabus does not ask for, and it is the biggest single block in the grade.

Grade 8 competency 5 reads "Uses a software package for physical computing to implement programming logic", and it is worth 5 of the grade's 30 periods. Its content column lists the components of a physical computing device, controllable devices, turning LEDs on and off, and designing LED patterns with simple programs. The learning outcome is "Writes a program to operate external circuits using two logic levels (yes/no, on/off)".

The game's lesson 5 is a logic gate lab: AND, OR and NOT truth tables. That is genuine ICT, it is well built, and students enjoy it. But truth tables are grade 10 O/L material. The words LED, sensor, actuator, physical computing and logic level return zero hits across the whole grade 8 file.

This also settles an open question from the other task

The grade 8 lesson 5 titles never matched: English said "Logic Gates", Sinhala said භෞතික ආගණනය. That was on the open-decisions list as a suspected translation error. It is not one. භෞතික ආගණනය reads as physical computing, which is exactly what the syllabus calls competency 5. The Sinhala title was right all along, and the English title is the one that drifted. One decision closed, at no cost.

3

What I would add

About 24 new activities and 5 new lessons, taking the game from 86 to roughly 110. Every one of them reuses an engine the game already has, so nothing new has to be invented to make them playable.

Grade 6 +2 lessons, +6 activities

  • L7 Software Around Us - sort games, media players and drawing software; match the place to the job the computer does there (school, bank, hospital, factory, farm); pick what needs software to work.
  • L8 Sound and Video - order the steps to record an audio clip; match audio and video file types; pick what video software can do.

Grade 7 +1 lesson, +7 activities

  • L2 gains a file-properties match: size, type, modified date.
  • L5 gains a variables activity and a find-the-bug activity, using the trace engine grade 8 already has.
  • L8 Safe, Legal and Kind Online - hacking, virus attacks, software piracy, cyber bullying, stealing data; trusted vs untrusted sites; online conferencing.

Grade 8 +1 lesson, +5 activities, 1 rebuilt

  • L5 rebuilt as Physical Computing - switch LEDs on and off with 1 and 0, order the steps to make a blink pattern, match sensors and actuators to what they do. The gate lab survives as a bonus round.
  • L3 gains an insert-a-table round.
  • L7 Apps on Phones and Smart Devices - what a mobile app is, where it runs, what it can sense.

Grade 9 +1 lesson, +5 activities

  • L2 gains COUNT and COUNTA, and a data-sorting activity.
  • L3 gains a nested-iteration trace and an array-variable challenge, both currently one true/false line each.
  • L7 ICT All Around Us - e-Commerce, m-Commerce, e-Health, e-Government, office automation, the digital divide.

The three new pages

You asked for a few new pages. These are the three that earn their place, drawn here in the project's own design system so you can see the shape. I specify them in this task; the redesign task builds them.

Syllabus Map

Every competency level in the grade, with the activity that covers it and a tick. This is the page that answers an examiner's first question, and it is the reason the whole mapping exists as data rather than prose.

Word Bank

Every ICT term in the game, English and Sinhala side by side, searchable. Useful to students in both media, and it doubles as the glossary that keeps the translation consistent across four grades.

Teacher Notes

Five decisions

Pick one per question and submit once at the bottom. My recommendation is first in each list and marked. Anything you leave blank, I will take my recommendation for.

O1 Grade 8 lesson 5, the logic gate lab

The lab is well made and students like it, but it teaches an O/L topic while the syllabus's biggest grade 8 block goes untaught. This is the only decision that touches work they already built.

Cost of being wrong: pick C and an examiner who opens the syllabus finds a 5 period block untaught behind a title that says otherwise. Pick B and they lose an activity for no syllabus gain.

O2 What "add a few pages" means

It could mean new lesson pages inside the game, or new pages in the app around the game. They are different work, so I would rather not guess.

Cost of being wrong: low either way. B and C are both subsets of A, so starting with A and cutting later is cheaper than the reverse.

O3 Which three app pages

Only relevant if O2 is A or C. Section 4 above draws all three.

Cost of being wrong: the Word Bank is the one with ongoing cost, because every new term has to be added in two languages. If translation review time is tight, B is the safe cut.

O4 How much new content

Sixteen competency levels have nothing at all; another four are covered by a single line. Closing everything is about 24 activities.

Cost of being wrong: A costs the most Sinhala review time from Ishini, since every new string needs her eye. C leaves grade 7 with 8 of 30 periods untaught.

O5 Sinhala for the new content

The new content has no Sinhala yet. The syllabus PDFs' Sinhala text layer is broken, so I will read the terms off page images rather than copying them, which is also how the original typos got in.

Cost of being wrong: it is her assessed project and she must be able to defend every Sinhala string, so anything unreviewed is a liability. Option A is the only one that keeps the draft and the reviewed state distinguishable.
