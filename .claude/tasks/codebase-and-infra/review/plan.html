The shape I want to build

Infra facts that shape this

Both platforms have limits that change the design rather than just the config, so they are here rather than buried in the task file.

ConstraintThe numberWhat it forces
Supabase Free active projects 2 max Across every org where you are Owner or Admin. Paused ones do not count. We do not know your current count, and that is question O4.
Supabase Free pausing 1 week idle Their wording is "a few user requests to the database each day over the previous week". Vague on purpose.
Vercel Hobby cron interval once per day A more frequent expression fails the deploy outright. Precision is per hour, plus or minus 59 minutes.
Vercel cron target 1 function A cron can only hit a Vercel Function, so the otherwise static site needs one api/ file. That does not force a build step.
Supabase Free database 500 MB Far more than nicknames and scores will ever need. Not a constraint here.

The honest version of the keep-alive. One ping a day is the most Vercel Hobby will allow, and it sits at the low end of what Supabase calls "a few requests a day". So the daily cron lowers the chance of a pause, it does not remove it, and I cannot promise otherwise. The real protection stays what the gate already chose: the leaderboard is device local first and syncs when the server answers, so a paused project degrades to a working offline board instead of a broken screen. Worth knowing because the risk lands in the school holidays, exactly when nobody is playing.

What I would do the moment you say go

Assuming the four picks above, in this order. Steps 1 to 4 are the ones that matter; everything after is ordinary work.

  • 1. git init plus a .gitignore that keeps .claude/, node_modules, .env* and the macOS ._* junk out.
  • 2. original/ - the 9 source files copied byte for byte. This is the parity baseline and it is never edited again.
  • 3. scripts/extract.mjs - parse the LESSONS literal out of each file, pair English with Sinhala by activity id, emit data/grade-6..9.json and data/adventure-9.json. Grade 9 Sinhala stays explicitly null rather than missing, so the gap is visible instead of silent.
  • 4. scripts/check-data.mjs - the test suite this project has never had. Same lessons, ids, types and answers as original/. It must be green before a single line of UI, because this is the step that can silently change an answer in a game children are graded on.
  • 5. tokens.css copied across, and the four fonts self-hosted with the weights cut to what DESIGN.md actually uses.
  • 6. App shell: hash router, state, language switch, grade picker, lesson path, activity, result.
  • 7. The 5 grade 6 activity types, with the 3 column symbol match fixed for phones, which is the one real mobile bug in the originals.
  • 8. Player and leaderboard against the storage adapter.
  • 9 to 12. Supabase schema with RLS and a validating submit_score(), PWA offline, vercel.json plus the keep-alive function, then deploy when you say go.

On the leaderboard and the law. Every player here is under 16, which is the child threshold in Sri Lanka's PDPA No. 9 of 2022. So the schema takes a nickname, an avatar and a class code, and nothing else. No real names, no email, no device fingerprint beyond a random id. Row level security is on with no anonymous writes at all; scores go in through a validating function that caps points and nickname length, and the leaderboard reads from views that expose nothing else. Ishini can delete any row.

Risks, and what is deliberately not here

  • Extraction is the dangerous step. A parsing slip changes an answer. check-data.mjs gates everything after it, and that is the whole reason it is step 4 rather than an afterthought.
  • Grade 9 has no Sinhala source. Those strings start empty and the app has to fall back to English rather than render a blank. Filling them is syllabus-enrichment's job or a teacher's, not mine to invent.
  • Supabase can still pause. Covered above. The offline board is the answer, not the cron.
  • Carried over from the redesign task and deliberately left out of this report: the Sinhala typo fixes T1 to T5, the grade 6 activity 1.3 distractor, and the grade 8 lesson 5 title. They are content decisions, they belong with syllabus-enrichment, and the data files can absorb them after extraction. Say the word if you would rather settle them here.
